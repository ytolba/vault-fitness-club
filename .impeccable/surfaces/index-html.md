**OWN-WORLD:** Near-black fields, warm metallic gold, warm white, architectural rules, narrow uppercase labels, generous negative space, and the supplied circular VAULT mark. The real nighttime exterior image supplies the halo; gold remains controlled, never decorative glitter.

**STORY:** See a real local place, understand Train / Restore / Repeat, verify concrete amenities, then call or get directions. No invented prices, memberships, reviews, staff, or performance claims.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A split architectural composition: oversized “Your hour. Your training. Your Vault.” at left; the supplied exterior photograph dominates the right. Open 24 hours and the address sit above two clear actions. A slim gold seam becomes the scroll marker.
